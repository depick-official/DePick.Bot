# Product

## Product

DePick

## Users

Community members use DePick inside Telegram and Discord to participate in prediction markets. Approved community moderators use the Custom Arena dashboard to create AI-prepared markets and monitor activity across their communities and channels.

## Product purpose

DePick gives communities a shared prediction-market experience using PICK. Custom Arena lets one approved moderator per community publish relevant markets within DePick-funded limits and track participation without exposing internal treasury or maker accounting.

## Brand personality

Direct, energetic, and trustworthy. Copy stays short and explains financial states plainly.

## Anti-references

Do not make moderator screens look like a separate enterprise admin product. Avoid decorative dashboards, unfamiliar controls, and visual patterns that conflict with the existing Custom Arena page.

## Design principles

- Reuse the existing DePick interaction and visual language.
- Keep the current task and available action obvious.
- Show community activity without exposing internal financial data.
- Explain unavailable actions with a useful reason.
- Prefer familiar mobile controls over new abstractions.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Maintain keyboard focus, readable contrast, touch-friendly controls, reduced-motion compatibility, and status text that does not rely on color alone.
